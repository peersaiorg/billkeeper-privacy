Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Applies to BillKeeper for Android, version 1.0.0 and later

In short: BillKeeper works entirely on your phone.
  • No account, no sign-in, no servers.
  • The app does not have Android's internet permission, so neither the app nor any library inside it can send data off your device.
  • We collect no personal data, show no ads, and use no analytics or tracking.
  • Your products, bills and settings stay in the app's private storage until you delete them.

1. Who we are

BillKeeper ("the app", "we") is a warranty and bill organiser published on Google Play by ShareAProfit. Questions about this policy: influbriz@gmail.com.

2. Information the app stores on your device

When you use BillKeeper, the following is saved only on your phone, inside the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read:

  • Product details you enter: name, brand, model, serial or IMEI number, category, purchase date, price, store, warranty period and notes.
  • Copies of the bill photos and PDF files you attach.
  • Your reminder choices and app settings (for example theme and reminder time).

This data is kept in an on-device SQLite database and a private files folder. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to access it.

3. Bill scanning (on-device text recognition)

The optional Scan & Auto-Fill feature reads a bill image or PDF using Google ML Kit text recognition with a model that is built into the app and runs on your phone. The image and the text it finds are used only to suggest values for the form, which you check before saving. They are not uploaded.

Google's ML Kit library can normally send anonymous usage diagnostics to Google. BillKeeper removes the internet permission from the app, so Android blocks any such transfer and nothing is sent.

Taking a bill photo opens Android's own camera app, and choosing a photo or PDF uses Android's own picker. BillKeeper only receives the single file you choose and does not hold camera, storage, location, contacts or microphone permissions.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, share or transfer your data, because we never receive it. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.

6. Backups

If you tap Export backup, the app creates a ZIP file containing your products, settings and bill files, and opens Android's share menu. You choose where it goes (for example Google Drive, email or your computer). Once you share it, that copy is handled by the service you picked under its own privacy policy. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private. Restore from backup only reads a ZIP file you select.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • Deleting a product removes its details, reminders and stored bill files.
  • Uninstalling the app, or using Clear storage in Android settings, deletes everything the app stored.
  • Backup ZIP files you exported are not removed automatically; delete them wherever you saved them.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on request.

8. Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox. We recommend using a screen lock on your phone. Exported backups are outside the app's protection, as described above.

9. Children

BillKeeper is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date, and the in-app policy will be updated in the same release.
